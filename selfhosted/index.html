What does self-hosting mean?

Self-hosting means running an app on a computer you control, such as a small home server, a cheap rented server or a Raspberry Pi, instead of using a company's online service. You keep your own data, you decide when to update, and many of these apps are free. The trade-off is that you look after the setup, the backups and the security yourself.

Before you install anything

Check the notes that come with each download, make sure the app is still being updated, and keep your server patched. Looking for ready-to-run apps for your phone or computer instead? Browse the ZER0 Vault.
