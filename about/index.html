About ZER0 Updates

ZER0 Updates is an independent, free website for football fans and people who like useful free software.

What you will find here

  • Football: headlines, league tables, fixtures and results for the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and the Champions League.
  • Downloads: small scripts written by us and open-source apps, each with plain-language instructions and honest notes about limits.
  • Deals, coupons and trending news: collected from public sources and refreshed automatically.

How the content is made

Football tables, fixtures, news, deals and coupons are gathered and updated automatically by software, several times a day. Headlines link to the original publisher, so you can read the full story at the source. We do not copy full articles. The tools on the Downloads pages are written and maintained by us, and the pages explaining them are written by hand.

How we make money

The site is free to use. It is supported by advertising from third-party networks and by affiliate links, which may pay us a commission when you buy something, at no extra cost to you. Paid placements are labelled as Sponsored. Ads and affiliate links do not decide what appears in the football tables or which tools we publish.

Accuracy and corrections

Automatic data can be late or wrong. League tables and scores come from third-party data providers, and prices and coupon codes can change at any time, so always confirm a price or code on the retailer's own site. If you spot a mistake, please tell us and we will fix it.
