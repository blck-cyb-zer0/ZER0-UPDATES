Help & Contact

How ZER0 Updates works, and how to reach us.

What is ZER0 Updates?

ZER0 Updates is a free site that brings together football news, trending stories, discounted deals, coupon codes and memes. Content is collected and summarised automatically by our bots.

How often is it updated?

News, deals and coupons refresh automatically once a day. Use the refresh button at the top of any section to fetch the latest version.

Are prices and coupon codes accurate?

Prices and codes are pulled automatically and can change or expire on the retailer's site at any time. Always confirm the price and the code before you buy.

How do downloads work?

Open the Downloads tab, tap Get to see the full details, then tap Download. A short ad plays first, then your file starts downloading automatically. Only download files you trust and have the right to use.

What are sponsored items and ads?

The site is supported by advertising and affiliate links. Paid placements are labelled as Sponsored, and some links may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you.

How do I use the ZER0 Assistant?

Tap the ZER0 Assistant button at the bottom right of the screen, type your question and send it. It can help you find things on the site.
