Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use zeroupdates.org. You do not need an account to use the site, and we do not ask for your name or email to browse or download.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It collects data such as your approximate location, device type, browser and the pages you view. This is aggregated and we do not use it to identify you.

Advertising and cookies

The site shows ads from third-party advertising networks. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, and some ads may open in a new tab or window. We do not control what third-party ads contain. You can limit ad cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalised advertising through your device or browser privacy controls.

Affiliate links

Some links to shops and services are affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The shop may place its own cookies when you click.

Local storage

Your browser stores a few settings on your device, such as your light or dark theme choice. This stays on your device.

ZER0 Assistant

If you use the assistant on the main site, the message you type is sent to our server and to an AI service so that a reply can be created. Please do not type personal or sensitive information into it.

Downloads

Our own scripts run entirely on your computer and, unless a page says otherwise, do not send your data anywhere. Third-party apps linked from our Downloads page are made by other developers and have their own privacy practices.

Third-party sites

We link to news publishers, shops and other sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? See our contact page.
